Not scoped on backend yet; see `docs/backend-briefs/119-httponly-session-cookies.md`.
- **Privacy + terms scaffold.** `064a010` stood up `/privacy` and `/terms` on torny-web as placeholder pages so the auth views could link them; `0696f59` wired the links from the CRM Sign-in and Register. Closes the first half of the compliance-floor track (`docs/production-readiness/08-compliance-floor.md`).

Shipped brief doc at `docs/backend-briefs/118-block-pdf-uploads-live.md`.
